## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, clinical precision, and emotional security required for a multigenerational family health ledger. Drawing from the intersection of Swiss/Minimalism and Soft UI Evolution, the aesthetic rejects cold, sterile hospital motifs in favor of an approachable, reassuring medical sanctuary.

- **Brand Personality**: Caring, meticulous, authoritative yet gentle, and deeply rooted in domestic harmony.
- **Target Audience**: Family caregivers, working parents managing children and aging parents, and health-conscious individuals who require rapid access and effortless long-term health tracking.
- **Emotional Response**: A calm sense of relief, trust, absolute privacy, and control during both daily wellness routines and critical medical moments.
- **Design Movement**: Soft UI Evolution balanced with Swiss functional rigor. Content is organized with clean typographic hierarchy, generous breathing room, structured metadata tags, tactile soft surfaces, and deliberate thumb-zone layout ergonomics for one-handed operation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, clinical warmth through earthy greens, restorative ivories, and grounding charcoal tones.

- **Primary (`#20594B`)**: Deep evergreen anchor representing clinical authority, heritage, and foundational trust. Used for primary navigation states, dominant actions, and key medical status indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2F6F5E`)**: Forest green accent applied to interactive elements, successful health metrics, filter selections, and active medication indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D97736`)**: Warm terracotta accent reserved for alerts, pending vaccine doses, reminder timestamps, and health notifications that need gentle urgency without inducing clinical panic.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1F2A2A`)**: Deep cypress slate for dominant headlines, critical diagnostic readouts, and primary body content, offering high contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#55615F`)**: Muted sage-slate for supportive metadata, historical timestamps, lab reference intervals, and inactive input labels.
- **Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas Background`: Warm ivory `#FAF8F5` preventing screen glare during nighttime hospital or bedside use.
  - `Card / Sheet Surface`: Crisp `#FFFFFF` providing structural separation and crisp clinical legibility.
  - `Borders & Dividers`: Soft parchment border `#E4E0DA` forming low-contrast boundaries that define hierarchy without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the editorial authority of **Newsreader** with the contemporary, diacritic-optimized clarity of **Be Vietnam Pro**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader)**: Deployed across screen headlines, member names, primary health summaries, and diagnostic categories. It communicates medical heritage, human care, and elevated craft.
- **Humanist Sans-Serif (Be Vietnam Pro)**: Formulated with exceptional Vietnamese tone-mark rendering and high legibility at micro-scales. Deployed for numerical metrics, dosage instructions, patient vitals, tables, and functional controls.
- **Rhythm & Optical Balance**: Display scales leverage generous line-heights to avoid glyph collisions in Vietnamese text. Numerical vitals utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) to keep diagnostic timelines and lab results vertically aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a mobile-first, one-handed operational model ("Thumb-Zone Architecture") that scales elegantly into structured columns on larger viewports.

- **One-Handed Reach (Thumb Zone)**:
  - Critical actions, primary filters, member switchers, and record creation buttons remain pinned within the lower 40% of the mobile viewport.
  - Informational headers, archival records, and static charts sit in the top view area for effortless scanning without hand repositioning.
- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. Interactive cards span the full 4 columns with strict internal padding (`16px` to `20px`).
  - **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `32px` margins and `24px` gutters, accommodating split views between family member directories and medical timelines.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at `1200px` max-width with `48px` outer margins. Left rail handles family profiles; the center rail houses chronological records; the right rail presents vitals and reminders.
- **Spacing Scale**: Strict multiples of 4px ensure consistent vertical cadence across multi-line Vietnamese content and multi-tiered clinical summaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using Soft UI evolution principles: subtle atmospheric offsets combined with warm parchment outlines.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Surface color `#FAF8F5`. Used for overall screen backgrounds, grouped form sections, and static chart bases.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px` stroke of `#E4E0DA` and a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(31, 42, 42, 0.06)`. This is the standard presentation for medical records, allergy tags, and medication items.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Tapped State)**: Background `#FFFFFF` with shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(31, 42, 42, 0.08)` and border `#D8D3CA`. Used for pinned bottom action sheets, date pickers, and floating action controls.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Overlays)**: Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(31, 42, 42, 0.12)` paired with a soft scrim overlay (`rgba(31, 42, 42, 0.3)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy ranges from `12px` to `20px`, softening clinical severity while maintaining Swiss structural precision.

- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-xl` / 16px - 20px)**: Primary health record containers, diagnostic detail cards, and family profile blocks use `16px` (up to `20px` for hero overview widgets), offering a comforting, tactile card feel.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs (`rounded-lg` / 12px - 16px)**: Form fields, action buttons, and bottom-sheet trays standardize on `12px` to `14px` for purposeful tap boundaries.
- **Pill / Badge Elements (`rounded-full`)**: Allergy alerts, vaccine completion tags, dosage counters, and family member avatars use full circular rounding for rapid visual parsing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#20594B` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `12px` border radius, minimum height `48px` to guarantee thumb hit targets. Pressed state: `#184439`.
- **Secondary / Soft Button**: Subtle surface fill `rgba(47, 111, 94, 0.08)`, text `#2F6F5E`, border `1px solid rgba(47, 111, 94, 0.16)`.
- **Ghost Action**: Transparent background with `#2F6F5E` text and underline or trailing chevron for navigational transitions.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E4E0DA`, and `16px` border radius.
- Padding is strictly `16px` (compact) or `20px` (standard).
- Divided internally with `1px` lines using `#E4E0DA` without heavy colored segmentations.

### Input Fields
- Container height `52px` to optimize one-handed input.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E4E0DA`, corner radius `12px`.
- Text typed in `#1F2A2A`, placeholder in `#55615F`.
- Focus state switches border to `#2F6F5E` with an outer halo of `0 0 0 3px rgba(47, 111, 94, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- Height `36px` to `40px` with `rounded-full` curvature.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E4E0DA`, text `#55615F`.
- Active: `#20594B` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(31, 42, 42, 0.08)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Sized at `22px x 22px` with a minimum tap target envelope of `44px x 44px`.
- Border `2px solid #55615F`, shifting to `#2F6F5E` fill with an inset white checkmark upon selection.

### Health-Specific Components
- **Family Member Switcher (Bottom-Docked)**: Horizontal scroll tray pinned above the navigation bar for frictionless one-handed profile toggling between dependents (e.g., child, parent, self).
- **Vitals Metric Tile**: Split metric presentation showing value in bold `Newsreader`, measurement unit in `Be Vietnam Pro Label`, and baseline trend indicator tagged with green (normal) or terracotta (attention).
- **Medication Schedule Tracker**: Row component featuring an immediate single-tap completion tick aligned to the right edge for natural thumb reach.
